### Fixed
- **Calibre import no longer files co-authors as aliases of the primary author** ([#1684](https://github.com/vavallee/bindery/issues/1684)). A book credited to two people made the second one an alias of the first, so every later book by that co-author was filed under their collaborator and they never got an author page at all. Which author survived depended on Calibre book id order, which is why whole back-catalogues appeared to vanish under a pen name. Co-authors are simply not recorded now (a Bindery book carries one author), and an import ignores an existing alias unless something backs it up, so libraries already affected recover on the next import. No alias rows are deleted, and author merges, spelling variants and non-latin alternate names keep resolving exactly as before. Leftover rows can be removed from the author page if you want them gone.
